## Overview

At 1Matrix, part of the Vietnam Blockchain Association, I worked on CRMatrix, the association's internal lead-to-deal CRM on Cloudflare Workers and D1. We were a team of three, and I took each epic from spec to plan, code, tests and pull request.

### What I did

- **Sales analytics** Built the dashboard of KPIs, funnel and stage-to-stage conversion for 4 roles, scoped on the server so each rep sees only their own data.
- **Full-text search** Moved contacts, leads, deals and companies from `LIKE` scans to ranked SQLite FTS5 search, indexed on every write.
- **Real-time notifications** A Durable Object per user that pushes notifications over hibernating WebSockets.
- **Configurable CRM** Pipeline stages, B2B types, lead sources and custom fields that admins manage without code changes.
- **AI-assisted delivery** Worked with AI coding agents and CodeRabbit reviews, and reviewed and tested what they produced.

More detail on the [CRMatrix project page](/project/crmatrix/).
