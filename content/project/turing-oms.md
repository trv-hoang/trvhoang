## Overview

Small online sellers take orders in chat apps and keep them in spreadsheets, notes and memory. At peak times, like Women's Day or Valentine's Day, orders get forgotten, especially made-to-order ones. Turing OMS puts every order in one place, from the chat to the money: quick capture with photos, customers, an order workflow, made-to-order production, stock, discounts, returns and receivables.

I lead it as product owner and lead developer. We work with a silk-flower shop as our design partner. The product is at the design stage; the code starts once the platform pieces it needs are in place.

### What I did

- **Discovery and prototype** Ran discovery with the design partner and validated the flows with a clickable prototype and a client demo.
- **One source of truth** Merged three specs into one approved PRD, with testable acceptance criteria on every user story and every architecture choice recorded as an ADR.
- **Platform first** Filed the platform features the product needs and built the core ones myself, merged within about three weeks: custom roles with record-level reach, audit outcomes, a workflow board, transition forms and barcode scanning.
- **Docs that stay honest** Every decision is marked as decided or open for review, and a checker fails on broken links, unknown story IDs and placeholders.
