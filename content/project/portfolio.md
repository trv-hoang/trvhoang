## Overview

My corner of the web: who I am, what I've built, what I write, and a map of every place I've eaten or had coffee. It is a static site built with Hugo and Tailwind CSS, hosted on Cloudflare Pages, in English and Vietnamese. I built it with AI coding agents and reviewed what they wrote.

### What I built

- **Light, fast pages** No front-end framework. Images are resized to the size they are shown at and served as WebP, so the home page loads in about 120 KB.
- **Motion with a purpose** Page transitions with the View Transitions API, a theme switch that wipes across the screen, and scroll reveals that respect reduced-motion settings.
- **The map** 230+ places on MapLibre with vector tiles, filters, my ratings, reviews and photos, and a "Where should I eat?" button that picks a well-rated place near you.
- **Place tooling** Scripts that add a place from a Google Maps link, re-check every place's position and opening status, and attach photos.
